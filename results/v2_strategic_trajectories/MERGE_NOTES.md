## What was tested

V2-E2 tested whether temporally resolved top-k Jacobian-Lens readouts could expose
the response-conditioned computation behind simple strategic choices. It used four
certified same-action matched pairs and isomorphic strategic/non-agentic framings.
This was a J-lens readout proxy, not a formal sparse nonnegative J-space analysis.

## What failed

Behavior and the matched contrasts succeeded. The monitoring objective did not: the
readout showed generic calculation/choice semantics and late answer preparation but
no coherent human-readable distinction between the changing decisive pathways. It
also supplied no compelling agent-specific readout across framings.

## What remains open

The result does not determine whether the relevant variables exist in residual
activations or participate in causal computation. Several correct short-CoT answers
state inaccurate expected values; that is an unquantified lead for a separate future
offline analysis, not evidence about CoT faithfulness. The A=8, B=1, C=3 label
imbalance is also a limitation for absolute action-preparation results.
