# V2-SWA-1 — strategic workspace atlas

Historical study label: `V2-E1`.

Status: **complete with mixed locked replication**.

## The question

When a model makes a strategic choice, can a generic “optimization” signal tell
us which specific strategy it is using?

## What happened

The locked study repeatedly found a prompt-absent generic optimization-token
signal at layer 43 in Kuhn and signaling games, before the final action. But the
stronger test failed: the signal did not reliably tell us the particular
strategy behind a shared action, and the same-action Kuhn bluff/thin/value
decoding was at chance.

So the result is mixed but easy to state: **we found a general decision signal,
not a readable strategic-intent signal**. It is observational and does not
reopen the failed V2 causal gate.

The locked study replicated a prompt-absent generic optimization-token signal
at layer 43 in Kuhn and signaling, before late action commitment. It did not
replicate the stronger five-game residual strategy advantage or same-action
Kuhn bluff/thin/value decoding.
